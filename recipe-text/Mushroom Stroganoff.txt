Step 3 – Fry it & Make it Saucy
Fry the onion until tender and slightly golden (~7 minutes).
Add the garlic and cook for a further 30 seconds.
Add the whisky to the frying pan and let it simmer until the liquid has reduced in half.
Add the flour, mix well until combined.
Pour in the vegetable stock and bring to the boil.
Add the mushrooms and mix to combine.
Add the coconut milk and mustard.
Mix and let simmer over low heat. (~10 minutes)

Step 4 – Bring it Together
