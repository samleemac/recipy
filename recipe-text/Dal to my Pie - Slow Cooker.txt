Step 1 - Preparation
Preheat your slow cooker.
Dice the onion and garlic.
Cut your potatoes and carrot into bite size chunks.
Drain and rinse the lentils.
Dice the halloumi and place in a bowl for later (once the meal has been in the slow cooker).



SLOW COOKER









Step 2 – Pop It In and Away You Go!
Once your slow cooker is ready and you have prepped your ingredients, place them all in the slow cooker.
Season with salt & pepper.
Stir well, pop the lid back on and leave it for ~3 hours (if on a high setting) or ~5 hours (if on a low setting).
If you are in the house, be sure to check on it and give a stir when you get a moment.

Step 3 – Fry it up!
Heat some oil in the frying pan.
Once the pan and oil are hot, place the halloumi in it. Season with salt & pepper.
Fry until golden on all sides, ensure to keep turning the halloumi to prevent them from burning. (~5 mins).
