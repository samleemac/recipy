Step 3 – Frying
Put your frying pan back on the heat and add a drizzle of oil.
Add the mushrooms until golden brown (~3-4 mins).
Add the garlic to the frying pan and stir.
Add the massaman curry paste to the frying pan and stir. 
Stir for 1 minute. 
Pour in the coconut milk and vegetable stock.
Add the peanut butter and stir well.
Mix and let simmer over low heat until the sauce has reduced by half. (~7-9 minutes)

Transfer the cashews to a bowl and put to the side for later.
