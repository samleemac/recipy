Method



Step 1 – Prep your Cauliflower

Cut your cauliflower into slightly bigger than bite size florets.
Place them into a mixing bowl together with salt and pepper and the mayonnaise. Coat the cauliflower.

Preheat your oven to 220°C.
Cut your cauliflower into slightly bigger than bite size florets.
Place them into a mixing bowl together with salt and pepper and the mayonnaise. Coat the cauliflower.

Step 2 – Crisp & Prep
Place the cauliflower nuggets onto a baking tray, spread into a single layer and put the tray into the oven.
Roast in the oven until golden (~25-30min).

In the meantime, dice the onion and garlic. Quarter the portobello mushrooms.

Step 3 – Simmer your stew
Heat a saucepan with a drizzle of oil. Add the onion and fry until golden (~4 min).
Stir in the portobello mushrooms, garlic and 1 tsp of Cajun seasoning, cook for another minute.
Add in the chopped tomatoes, vegetable stock paste and water.
Bring the stew to a simmer, then lower the heat and allow to simmer until thickened (~5 minutes).

Drain and rinse the butter beans, put 2/3 of them straight into the saucepan, crush the remaining 1/3 before placing them into the saucepan as well. Cook for another ~5 minutes until thickened.

Step 4 – Bring it Together
Add chili to the saucepan, mix well. 
Then remove the saucepan from the heat.
Once the nuggets are crispy, remove them from the oven.
Serve the spicy bean stew into bowls before adding the cauliflower nuggets on top.


Enjoy!
