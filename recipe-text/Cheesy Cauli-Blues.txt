Method




Step 1 - Preparation
Boil the kettle.
Preheat the oven to 220°c.
Grate the cheddar cheese and add to a bowl.
Add the blue cheese and breadcrumbs to the same bowl.
Season with salt & pepper.
Stir in 2 tbsp of olive oil and set aside.
Cut the cauliflower into small florets.

Step 4 – Bring it Together
Remove the cauliflower from the oven and mix the pasta into it.
Stir the cheese sauce into the oven dish. 
Sprinkle the blue cheese and breadcrumb mix on top.
Place back in the oven until the crumb is golden (~5 mins)
Remove from the oven and serve.

Step 3 – Make it Saucy
In another saucepan, heat 2 tbsp of oil.
Once the oil is hot, add in the flour and stir until it forms a paste.
Gradually add in the water to the paste along with the vegetable stock. Bring to a boil.
Stir and simmer until the sauce has thickened. (~2mins)
Stir in the cream and remove from the heat. 
Add the cheddar cheese to the pan and stir until melted. 
Return the pan to the heat and add in the spinach.  
Once the spinach has wilted, remove from heat.

Step 2 – Cook & Roast
Cook the pasta according to the package instructions. (once cooked, drain the pasta)
Put the cauliflower into an ovenproof dish and drizzle oil over the top. Season with salt & pepper to taste. 
Put in the oven until golden brown. (~15-18 mins)
