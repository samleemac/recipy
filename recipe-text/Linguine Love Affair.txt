Step 2 – Pasta & Mushrooms
Cook the pasta according to the package instructions.
Heat the oil in a frying pan.
Add the breadcrumbs and almonds.
Fry until toasted (~3 mins).
Remove the breadcrumbs and almonds and place on a separate plate for later.

Step 4 – Bring it Together
Turn the heat off for the frying pan.
Season with salt and pepper.
Mix well and serve.
Sprinkle the toasted breadcrumbs and almonds.



Enjoy!

Step 3 – Fry it & Make it Saucy
Put the now empty frying pan back on the heat and add oil.
Add the onion marmalade and garlic and stir for 1 minute.
Add the stock to the frying pan and bring to the boil to reduce (~4 mins.).
Add the peas, cream, and pepper to taste and stir for 2 minutes.
Drain the linguine and add to the frying pan.
Add the hard Italian style cheese.
