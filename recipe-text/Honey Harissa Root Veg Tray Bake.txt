Method



Step 1 – Get Preppin’ 
Pre-heat the oven to 200°C
Boil the kettle.
Cut the carrots and parsnip into batons (~1cm wide and 5cm long).
Cut the red onion into wedges.
In a bowl, mix the harissa paste, 15g honey, and olive oil.
Season with salt and pepper to taste.

Pre-heat the oven to 200°C
Boil the kettle.

Step 2 – Let’s Roast, Honey
Put the carrots, parsnip, and red onion onto a baking tray.
Drizzle olive oil and season with salt and pepper.
Coat the vegetables and spread into a single layer on the tray.
Put the tray into the oven for ~10 minutes.

Step 4 – Bring it Together
Once the bulgur wheat has cooked through, stir in the butter, and fluff up with a fork. 
Dish up into bowls, place the roasted vegetables on top, drizzle your sauce with the harissa paste, crumble on your feta and finally scatter on some almond flakes.

Enjoy!

Step 3 – Well Bulgur Me Up
Once the water from the kettle has boiled, pour the water into a saucepan together with the stock paste, stir well.
Put in the bulgur wheat and cranberries and bring to a boil again.
Simmer for 1 minute, remove from heat and set aside for ~12-15 minutes.

Turn the vegetables in the oven and place back for another ~7 minutes.
Drizzle 15g honey over the vegetables before placing back in the oven for the last 5 minutes.
