Step 1 – Get it Ready
Preheat the oven to 200 degrees C.

Step 2 – Keep it Moist
In another bowl, mix the almond milk, olive oil, lemon juice, and vanilla extract.
Slowly pour in the mixture of the wet ingredients into the large bowl with the dry ingredients, mixing until combined.
Once the batter has been combined, add in the blueberries.
Gently fold them in, be careful not to overmix or mix too hard.

Step 3 – Separate the Berries
With a tablespoon, spoon your batter into the muffin cups right to the top.
Bake for ~15 minutes or until lightly golden on the top.
Ensure that they are fully cooked through by inserting a knife/toothpick into the centre of the muffin and seeing if it comes out clean.
Allow to cool before transferring to an airtight container or even eating!
