Halou -Bean Tacos🇲🇽


DID YOU KNOW?
“Black beans are like secret agents of nutrition: ordinary-looking but packing a stealthy punch of protein and fiber. They're always ready to make your meal un-bean-lievably healthy!”


Ingredients
1 tin of black beans
250g of halloumi
1 tbsp lime juice
1 tbsp tomato puree
2 tbsp mayonnaise
1 head of baby gem lettuce
4 corn tortillas
100ml water
2 tsp chipotle paste
3 cloves of garlic
Nutrition – Total / Per Serving
Calories: 1482kcal / 741kcal
Carbohydrates: 180g / 90g
Protein: 34g / 17g
Fat: 66g / 33g
Fibre: 30g / 15g
Sugar: 8g / 4g

MethodStep 1 - Preparation
Preheat the oven to 180°C.
Chop the baby gem lettuce and halloumi into bite size chunks.
Drain the black beans and place in a bowl and use a fork/potato masher to mash the beans slightly (there can be some whole beans left).
Peel and dice the garlic.










Step 2 – Bean Time
Heat some oil in the saucepan, once hot add the garlic and tomato puree. Cook for 1 minute.
Add in the water and the half-mashed black beans. Mix well and then let it cook for around 4 minutes.





Step 3 – Fry it Up!
Mix 1 tsp of chipotle paste into the black bean mixture after the original 4 minutes before cooking the sauce some more for another 3 minutes.
Heat up the frying pan and add some oil. Once hot add the halloumi and fry until golden.
Place the corn tortillas into the oven.
Step 4 – Bring it Together
Mix the mayonnaise and remaining chipotle paste in a small bowl.
Take the saucepan and frying pan off the heat and remove the tortillas from the oven.

Unleash your inner child and build your tacos as you wish!

Don’t forget a drizzle of lime juice over your tacos for the finishing touch!
