Method




Step 1 - Preparation
Preheat the oven to 200°c.
Halve, peel, and thinly slice the red onion.
Peel and dice the garlic gloves.
Cut the halloumi into 2cm squares.
Drain and rinse the lentils. 
Halve the tomatoes and tear the ciabatta into roughly 2cm chunks.

Step 3 – Harissa the lentils
Take out the tomatoes and ciabatta. 
Add the water and vegetable stock into the frying pan.
Bring to the boil then reduce the heat and simmer. (~2mins)
Add the lentils and harissa paste to the frying pan.
Bring the pan back to the boil and cook. (~2mins)
Add the spinach and cook until wilted. (~2mins)
Take off the heat and add the tomatoes, set aside to rest.

Step 2 – Bake & Fry
Place the tomatoes and ciabatta on a baking tray and drizzle some oil on top. Add salt & pepper if desired. 
Place them in the over until the ciabatta is golden. (~8-10mins)
Meanwhile, heat oil in a frying pan and add the red onion. 
Season the red onion with salt & pepper. 
Fry the red onion until softened. (~7-8 mins)
Add the garlic and cook for another minute.
