Step 3 – Finishing Touches

Add a small splash of water to loosen up the mixture (you want to be able to drizzle it!).
Place the tortillas onto a baking tray and place in the oven. They only want to be warmed up, so 1-2 minutes should be plenty.

Step 2 – Fry it up!
Heat some oil in a frying pan.
Once hot, add the mushrooms and cook until they have softened and started to colour (~5 min).
Then stir in the garlic, tomato puree, kidney beans (both whole and crushed), and Cajun seasoning.
Mix well before adding in the vegetable stock.
Stir to mix and then reduce the heat to simmer for ~5 mins.
Season with salt & pepper if required.

Step 4 – Building Time!
Place the warmed up tortillas on a place, and build your tacos as you wish!
You could try this order: lettuce first, a healthy spoonful of the bean and mushroom mixture, finishing up with the cheese and a drizzle of the mayonnaise.


Enjoy!
