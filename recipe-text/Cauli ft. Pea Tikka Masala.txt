Ingredients
400g cauliflower
150g jasmine rice
2 tsp ground cumin
2 cloves of garlic
15g flaked almonds
4 tbsp tikka masala paste
300g tomato passata
200g peas
2 tbsp red chili jam
2 tbsp butter
150ml cream**
1 vegetable stock paste

MethodStep 1 – Preparation & Roast
Boil the kettle and preheat the oven to 220°C.
Cut the cauliflower into small bite size chunks. Place onto a baking tray and season with salt & pepper and drizzle with oil.
Spread into a single layer and sprinkle 1 tsp of cumin before putting into the oven.
Grate the garlic.









Step 2 – Rice & Flavour Time
Cook the rice according to the package instructions.
Heat the frying pan, once hot add in the flaked almonds. Stir continuously until they are lightly toasted (~3 minutes). Remove from the pan and set aside in a small bowl.
Place the frying pan back on heat and heat up some oil. Add the tikka paste, garlic and 1 tsp cumin. Stir-fry for 30 seconds.





Step 3 – Make it Saucy
Add the tomato passata, vegetable stock, and cream to the frying pan. Bring the sauce to a boil before reducing the heat to a simmer. It should be ~4 minutes before the sauce has thickened, remember to stir occasionally.

Once the sauce has thickened stir in the peas, chili jam, and butter. Remove the cauliflower from the oven and add into the sauce. Season with salt & pepper.
Step 4 – Bring it Together
Fluff up the rice before serving.

Spoon over the sauce and sprinkle 
your dish with the toasted flaked 
almonds.


Enjoy!

Top tip: Add a dash of lime juice to the rice to give it some extra OOMPH!
