Step 2 – Cook Your Potatoes & Make the Filling
Add the potatoes into a saucepan with the boiling water and cook until tender.
Meanwhile, heat some oil in a frying pan. Add the mushrooms and fry until brown (~5 minutes).
Add the soy sauce to the frying pan and mix for 1 minute before adding the garlic and sundried tomatoes.
Season with salt and pepper before adding the tomato passata, red wine, vegetable stock, and lentils. 
Bring to a boil before reducing the heat to a simmer, Let simmer for ~9 minutes or until the mixture has thickened.
